About AzeeTech System

Revolutionizing the way restaurants interact with food lovers.

AzeeTech is an all-in-one Smart Menu Ecosystem designed for modern cafes, hotels, and food courts. We completely replace outdated paper menus with dynamic, beautiful interactive digital interfaces.

Our platform helps restaurant owners manage their entire business workflow digitally—from generating stylish table-specific QR codes to managing menu pricing dynamically and dispatching orders directly to the kitchen without manual errors.

Amazing Core Features

Everything you need to turn your regular cafe into a smart tech-driven dining hub.

🖨️ Table QR Generation

Create independent, elegant QR codes for individual tables instantly. Customers simply scan, browse, and place their orders directly.

🍴 Live Menu Manager

Take full control of your menu. Add new dishes, remove items, change prices, or organize categories in real-time with zero downtime.

👨‍🍳 Live Kitchen Sync

Keep your kitchen staff fully synchronized. Customer orders hit the kitchen display panel instantly, well-organized by table number.

🛍️ Hybrid Billing Counter

Manage traditional on-table dining and direct takeaway/home parcels simultaneously from a single, unified administrative terminal screen.

📊 Real-time Daily Analytics

Track real-time counter metrics, net daily earnings, and item sold volume. Instantly export sales history leaderboard sheets directly to CSV files.

🔐 4-Layer Security Gate

Complete data protection shield. Restrict unauthorized staff access on critical modules like Waiter PIN configs, Daily Sell Reports, and Live Billing data using secure owner authentication codes.
